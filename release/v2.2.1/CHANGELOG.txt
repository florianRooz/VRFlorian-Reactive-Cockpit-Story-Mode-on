VRFLORIAN REACTIVE COCKPIT — CHANGELOG
=======================================

VERSION 2.2.1 — OPEN TEST
-------------------------

- Fixes altitude-limited planetary atmosphere music remaining active after
  Elite stops publishing surface coordinates above the authored limit.
- Installs Florian's final Mission 2 objective wording and audio-level polish,
  including consistent naming of Muni in the post-survey clue.
- Removes the obsolete Mission 2 completion-triumph playback.
- Repairs the PowerShell updater's process check so later releases can install
  automatically without a manual bootstrap.
- Preserves V2.2.0 mission progress, cockpit assignments and configuration.

VERSION 2.2.0 — OPEN TEST
-------------------------

- Ships as a clean first-time-player build with no Commander identity,
  cockpit assignments, machine paths, mission progress or gameplay logs.
- Includes Reactive Cockpit 1.10.0-DISCO-TEST and launcher 0.7-OPEN-TEST.
- Adds Story Mode ON with Living World, Mission 1 and Mission 2 selection.
- Adds the player-facing Story Reader with one current clue, scanner status,
  operational windows and scan progress where relevant.
- Adds the Story Platform 0.12.2 live-location gate. Cached or menu-time
  location data cannot start story audio; a fresh in-game Location, FSDJump or
  CarrierJump event with a real StarSystem opens the gate.
- Includes Florian's final Mission 1 and Mission 2 graphs for this release.
- Forces player builds to use their own local Story Platform\story_assets map,
  never an authoring-machine or old Story Director 4.0 path.
- Adds launcher update checks and SHA-256-verified in-place installation while
  preserving cockpit assignments, configuration, mission progress and logs.
- Adds CTRL+SHIFT+D Disco Mode with two-second lighting palette changes and
  correct restoration of the current cockpit/story state when switched off.
- Preserves the complete Classic Elite cockpit and all bundled cockpit themes.
